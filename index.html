A Timeless Tea Experience

Crafted with warmth, elegance, and artisanal care

Inspired by the delicate beauty of heirloom china and wild white roses, Cherotea Rose offers an unhurried retreat from the everyday. Every pot is steeped to order from whole-leaf botanicals and paired with warm buttermilk scones, Devonshire-style clotted cream, savory finger sandwiches, and petite patisserie.
